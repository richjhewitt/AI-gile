# Ways of working: solo coder

A plain starting point for {owner}, coding alone (or with a few people) and no AI. It is a local file, never in git: change it any time.

## The board
- **Backlog**: ideas and jobs not started. Keep it short; park the rest.
- **To do**: what is next. Three cards at most.
- **Doing**: the card you are on. **One at a time.**
- **For you / Test**: what is built and waits for you to check.
- **Done**: finished and checked.

## One card at a time
- Pick the top card in To do. Move it to Doing. Finish it, then check it.
- Stuck for more than 30 minutes? Add a comment saying what you tried, and move on to the next card.
- Something new turns up while you work? Make a card for it. Do not do it now.

## Writing a card
- A short title that says the job: "Fix login redirect", not "Login stuff".
- Under it, bullet points, one idea per line.
- Add test steps: what to click and what you should see.

## Bugs
- Raise the bug with what you did, what you saw and what you expected.
- Fix the bug first when it blocks other work, otherwise put it in To do.

## Small habits
- Commit small, with a message that says why.
- At the end of the day write one line on the card you were on: where you stopped and what is next.
- Weekly: clear Done, and look at what is left in Backlog.
