The one big idea

The phone is a window onto your board at home

  • Your board on the PC keeps the cards, the chats and the keys.
  • The phone shows them and lets you act.
  • Lose the phone? Remove it on the PC. Nothing is lost.

Offline, then catching up

  1. No signal? You still see everything as it was, with the time it was last updated.
  2. Reply, pass or send back as normal. Each shows Waiting to send.
  3. Signal back: they send in order, by themselves.
  4. If the board changed meanwhile, the board wins, and the phone tells you what happened.

Done when: three actions made in flight mode all reach the PC in the right order once back online, and none are lost or sent twice.

Honest comparison

What we do better than Discord

  • Calm by default. One kind of alert is on: things that wait on you.
  • Grouped alerts. "3 things", not three pings. Quiet hours on from day one.
  • Chat sits on the work. A talk about a card is on the card. A Discord thread is not a task.
  • Your AI teams are in the chat list, next to your friends.
  • Friend chats are private end to end. Discord text chats are not (check before we say it in public).
  • Your own machines. No account with us, no ads, no upsells, no streaks.
Honest comparison

Where Discord is better, and stays better for now

  • Voice and video calls.
  • Big communities and servers with thousands of people.
  • A huge choice of bots and add-ons.
  • Years of polish, and it works on every device.

We don't try to beat these in the first version.

Keeping it small

What we won't do in version 1

  • No voice or video.
  • No group chats.
  • No reactions, typing dots or read receipts.
  • No moving cards by dragging.
  • No editing projects or teams. Do that on the PC.
  • No reaching a friend's board.
  • No checking every second. It drains the battery.
  • No iPhone yet.
Safety first
Your PC
board, cards, chats, keys
⇅
Phone
a window, paired and signed

Nothing gets weaker on the PC

  • Today the board only answers on the PC. That stays true for everything else.
  • Only a paired phone gets in, and every request it makes is signed.
  • Alerts say only "something new". Details come over the safe link, never through Google.
  • This part gets a security review before any building starts.
For testers

The nine checks, on one page

  1. Paired in 30 seconds; Remove on the PC locks the phone out.
  2. For you on the phone matches the PC exactly.
  3. Pass goes to Done; Send back returns with your line.
  4. One tap posts the exact answer and clears it from For you.
  5. Phone and PC show the same chats; friend replies have 🔒.
  6. Five cards in a minute make one alert; answers work from the alert.
  7. A bug lands within a minute, with screenshot, phone model and version.
  8. Nothing buzzes in quiet hours or tired mode; one morning summary.
  9. Offline actions all arrive, in order, once, when back online.
