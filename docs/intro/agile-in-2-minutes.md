# 🎓 Agile in 2 minutes

Eight short slides. No exam at the end.

---

## 1. 🐾 The idea: small steps, shown often

- Big plans go wrong in big ways. Small steps go wrong in small ways, and you notice sooner.
- So the work is cut into bite-sized cards, and each one is shown to you as soon as it's built.
- **Agile** is the name for working like this. That's the whole secret.

*In AI-gile: "a new board" is too big for one card. "A lightbulb button to jot down an idea" is the right size.*

---

## 2. 🗂️ The columns: where every card lives

- **Backlog**: ideas and wishes, in no rush. **To do**: picked for now.
- **In progress**: a team is building it. **For you to test**: built, waiting for your verdict.
- **Done**: you said yes. Cards move left to right; the team moves them, you make the last move.

*In AI-gile: a purple diamond next to a project means something there is waiting for you.*

---

## 3. 🧭 You decide, the team builds

- You say what you want and what matters most. The team works out how.
- Before starting, the lead suggests a plan and asks. Press **👍 Go** and they're off.
- Stuck on a choice? They ask you a question rather than guess.

*In AI-gile: questions land in **For you**, often with quick buttons so you can answer in one tap.*

---

## 4. 🏃 Sprints: a short burst with something to try

- A **sprint** is a short, focused burst of work: a handful of cards that belong together.
- It always ends in something you can try, not a progress report.
- Then you look, react, and the next sprint starts from what you learned.

*In AI-gile: "Sprint M1: a pretend friend on this machine" ended with a second window you could click around in.*

---

## 5. 📣 Stand-ups: done, next, stuck

- A **stand-up** is a two-minute check-in. Each team answers three things: what's done, what's next, what's in the way.
- Anything in the way is a **blocker**, and blockers come first, because they stop everything.
- No meeting room needed. Nobody even has to stand.

*In AI-gile: press **📣 Stand-up all**, then read the answers in **📋 Stand-up report**.*

---

## 6. 🧪 Tests and accepting: your call

- When a card reaches **For you to test**, it comes with steps: what to open and what you should see.
- Happy? **✅ Accept** and it goes to **Done**. Not quite? **🔁 Send back** and say what you saw.
- Sending back is not failure. It's the system working exactly as designed.

*In AI-gile: "The button is there, but it's hiding behind the menu" is a perfectly good comment.*

---

## 7. 🌙 When you're away

- Off to bed or out for the day? Press **🌙 Going quiet** and every working team knows.
- The teams carry on with what already has a Go, and save their questions for later.
- The **🌙 Night watchman** keeps an eye on things and passes your messages on. Press **☀️ I'm back** to pick up again.

*In AI-gile: you can wake up to three cards in **For you to test**, each with a short note.*

---

## 8. 🛟 Where to get help

- **🎓 Tour**: a guided lap of the board, at the pace you choose.
- **💬 Ask the team**: send a question; the answer comes back to your messages.
- **💡 Got an idea?**: the lightbulb at the top. Jot it down, sort it out later.

*In AI-gile: there are no silly questions. The teams are machines; they don't sigh.*
