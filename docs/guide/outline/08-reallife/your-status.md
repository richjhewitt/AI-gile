Tell the teams if you're around, busy, or taking the day off.

- Click the 🟢 in the top bar (under **More ▾** if you unpinned it): Online, Idle, Do not disturb, Offline or 🛌 Rest day
- 🛌 Rest day: the night watch takes over, only urgent asks stay in your inbox, teams ask the Lead Scrum Master instead of you
- Pick Online to end it: the rest comes back, with a summary of what happened
