🧪

One pile for you

For you holds everything waiting on you, across every project: questions, things to test, tasks only a human can do. Your queue says what's next and how long it takes.

🌙

Teams that work while you sleep

Each project has a lead and a team of AI agents. Comment on a card and the team wakes up, does the work, and hands it back with steps a tired human can follow.

🤖

An AI tester checks first

Before anything reaches you, a tester follows the steps in a copy of the app and shows what it saw, with a screenshot. Flick through the passed ones and accept in a click.

🔍

Proof, not promises

Every hand-over shows what changed: the files, the diff, the screenshots. No opening every app to see if it's done.

💬

Chat with your leads

A Discord-style rail: one icon per project, purple diamonds for what needs you. Message a lead an idea; they turn it into cards and a plan, with a ✅ Go button.

🧹

Keeps itself tidy

Related cards fold into one, muddled titles get fixed, and a token check keeps the code lean. Fewer things to switch between, less to think about.

🔒

Yours, on your computer

  • The board runs on your own machine and only answers you.
  • Private DMs between people are end-to-end encrypted.
  • No accounts, no trackers, no cookies.
🌍
