What we keep

Short version: only what you send us, and not for long.

  • Only what you send us: what you type into a form, like your email. Nothing else about you.
  • It is sealed so our server can't read it: in your browser before it leaves (on an older browser, the moment it arrives). Only our own board can open it.
  • It is deleted from the server as soon as it reaches our board. Anything still there after 30 days is deleted anyway.
  • We keep it on our board only until we've replied to you, then delete it there too.
  • No trackers, no cookies, no analytics.
  • Your language and light or dark choice stay in your own browser. They are never sent to us.

Want it gone sooner? Reply to our email and say so.

The AI part

  • AI-gile is the board. The work is done by AI models from other companies, like Claude, Codex and Gemini, on your own accounts.
  • What they write and do comes from them, and it can be wrong. Check it before you rely on it or ship it.
  • They change files and run commands in the folders you connect. Keep a backup.
  • What you give a model goes to the company that runs it, under their terms. Each one's own terms and costs apply.
  • AI-gile is an early test version, given as it is.
